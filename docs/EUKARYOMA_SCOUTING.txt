EUKARYOMA SCOUTING (2026-10-29)
================================
WHAT: https://eukaryoma.unil.ch = OMA-browser instance (SIB/UNIL Dessimoz lab), release "All.Oct2026"
 (API /api/version/: oma_version All.Oct2026, api_version 1.11). Page title "EukaryOMA Database",
 "specialized database for inference of orthologs among eukaryotic genomes".
SIZE: 1328 eukaryotic species (all codes X0001..., 1328 lines in oma-species.txt; names/taxids look EukProt-style),
 33,776,005 proteins; 1,564,658 OMA groups (11.76M proteins, 34.8%); 1,567,736 deepest-level HOGs (17.80M proteins, 52.7%).
 No prokaryotes. Method: OMA ecosystem / FastOMA (Majidian et al. Nat Methods 22:269-272, 2025, doi:10.1038/s41592-024-02552-8)
 is the engine listed; the site itself does not state the exact pipeline (inferred, not confirmed).

 Site says cite: Altenhoff et al. NAR 52:D513 (2024) doi:10.1093/nar/gkad1020 and FastOMA above. ASK LEAD for a dedicated reference.

USE: (1) independent orthology (FastOMA HOGs, different algorithm and species sampling) to cross-validate Ancestra gains/losses at
 M/P nodes: map our proteins by exact sequence/ID if EukProt-sourced, else by taxid/species; compare HOG presence at matching
 taxonomic levels (Metazoa, Choanozoa, Opisthokonta, Streptophyta...). (2) Denser sampling around transitions, esp. choanoflagellates,
 ichthyosporeans, aphelids, additional sponges/placozoa, red algae, brown algae; could supply stand-ins for missing Volvocales/Zygnematophyceae
 only partly (those clades are poorly covered). (3) Caveats: need sequences for ID-mapping (seqs file 404 -> use API /api/genome/CODE/proteins/
 or request); the OrthoXML gives gene IDs only; many transcriptome-based species (EukProt) -> missing-gene bias; no dedicated citation yet.
