MARCOTTE LECA INTERACTOME -- DATA SCOUTING (2026-10-29)
=====================================================

1. PAPER
Title : Ancient eukaryotic protein interactions illuminate modern genetic traits and disorders (bioRxiv preprint, 2024-05-29; PMC11160598, PMID 38853926, DOI 10.1101/2024.05.26.595818)
Peer-reviewed version: "A protein interactome for the last eukaryotic common ancestor illuminates the biochemical basis of modern genetic diseases", Cell Genomics 6(6):101254 (2026-05-27), DOI 10.1016/j.xgen.2026.101254, PMID 42202782, PMC13261697.
Authors: Cox RM, Papoulas O, Shril S, Lee C, Gardner TP, [Ansari ZT in published version], Battenhouse AM, Lee M, Drew K, McWhite CD, Yang D, Leggere JC, Durand D, Hildebrandt F, Wallingford JB, Marcotte EM (UT Austin).
NOTE: this is NOT an animal-focused paper. It reconstructs the LECA interactome only (conserved across eukaryotes); no
metazoan-specific / multicellularity analysis. (The Marcotte-lab human map, Drew et al. 2021 Mol Syst Biol, is a different paper.)

What it did
- LECA gene set: Dollo parsimony over 156 UniProt/QfO reference proteomes (incl. archaea/bacteria outgroups), eggNOG orthogroups (ENOG50xxxx, eukaryote level) -> 10,092 LECA OGs (also DTL-reconciliation sets DTL70/DTL90, Wagner set). 4,777 human-gene-containing OGs (~9,908 human genes).
- CF-MS: integrated raw data of 31 species, 149 separations, 10,491 fractions (SEC/IEX/IEF etc.), 379.8M peptides, 259,732 OGs/unique proteins; plus ~15,000 AP-MS/BioID/RNA-pulldown experiments (total 26,297 MS experiments). New data for rotifer (Brachionus), Euglena, Phaeodactylum, Tetrahymena, pig trachea, Xenopus sperm. Reprocessed raw files; PRIDE accessions are in STAR-methods resource_summary.xlsx (947 rows).
- Filter: OGs with >=150 PSMs -> 5,989 OGs with elution profiles. Profiles are per ORTHOGROUP (ENOG), not per protein.
- Model: supervised (LinearSVC chosen; ExtraTrees, SGD compared; TPOT) trained on 1,499 known complexes (CORUM/ComplexPortal), stratified; 10% FDR.
- Result: 109,466 pairwise PPIs among 3,193 OGs; walktrap hierarchical clustering into 199 ... 2,014 complexes (cuts 199,318,398,437,557,676,796,1202,1608,2014); supergroup-presence flags per OG (amor_11, exca_2, tsar_5, viri_13 columns = number of species-experiments? supergroup support).
- Findings: recovers/extends vesicle-tethering complexes (GARP/EARP+EIPR1, HOPS/CORVET, TRAPP incl. TRAPPC12 pan-eukaryotic, COG+TMF1/RNF20-40), ARP2/3 + phagocytosis machinery (supports phagocytic LECA), disease prediction (EFHC2 ciliopathy, ATP6V1A osteopetrosis, GLG1 and short-rib dysplasia). Nothing on animal/multicellular-specific complexes: by design ONLY LECA-conserved OGs enter the map.

4. OVERLAP WITH OUR 187 PROTEOMES (exact taxid match)
In both (16): Homosap, Caenele, Drosmel, Nemavec, Stropur [5 animals, all in T_metazoa M]; Dictdis [T_dictyostelia]; Arabtha, Ceraric, Selamoe [land plants; embryophyte origin if coded]; Chlarei (unicellular sister of plants), Sacccer (yeast; unicellular/reversal), Euglgra, Phaetri, Tetrthe, Trypbru (taxid 185431 vs T. brucei 927 5702 -> same species, check), Plasfal (taxid 36329 strain vs 5833) -- the last 6 are unicellular outgroups.
Same-species-but-absent: mouse, pig, Xenopus laevis, rotifer, maize/rice/wheat/soy/tomato/etc.: not in our set; but congruent lineage nodes (Bilateria, Embryophyta) are covered by others; map at OG level (below).
Multicellular origins covered: T_metazoa (5 species, M-side only; sister side NOT sampled -> cannot do within-CF-MS animal vs choanoflagellate comparison); T_dictyostelia (1 sp); land plants (not a listed transition here; Chlamydomonas only). Fungi transitions: only Sacccer. So CF-MS comparisons across independent origins are essentially impossible; only the ANIMAL stem is addressable, and only by OG origin/retention, not elution.
Mapping to our HOGs: three routes: (a) TableS1 gives UniProt accessions of each ENOG in ~30 species; our proteomes are mostly UniProt reference (UP...) proteomes, so accession -> our protein -> Ancestra HOG works directly for human/dros/worm/nemve/urchin/Dicty/yeast/Arabidopsis/Chlamy/Phaeo/Tetrahymena/Euglena/Trypanosoma (accession version / canonical-isoform caveats; EukProt-sourced species differ). (b) Elution rows are ENOG OGs, so the whole complexome lives at ENOG level: HOG->ENOG via shared proteins (many-to-many; splits/merges must be handled). (c) human gene names in clustered table for sanity checks.

5. PROPOSED ANALYSES (<10 h)
A. Complex membership vs stem-gain at transition nodes (cheap, ~1-2 h). Map the 3,193 complexome OGs (and cut_437/cut_1202 complexes) to our HOGs through human/Dros/Nemve accessions; classify each by Ancestra origin node (LECA, Opisthokonta, Holozoa, Metazoa stem ...). Test: are subunits whose HOG appears at Metazoa stem (or expanded paralogs at Metazoa stem) over-represented in particular complexes, vs a permutation null preserving complex size? Caveat: the map contains ONLY LECA OGs by construction, so "new" Metazoa subunits are absent; what is testable is paralog expansion/duplication of LECA OGs at the stem (HOG-level duplications at Metazoa node) and loss/retention. Metazoa-gained subunits must come from other sources (CORUM/ComplexPortal, our PPI-from-structure data).
B. Co-retention of complex subunits across lineages (~3 h). For each complex, compute pairwise phylogenetic-profile correlation / fraction of members present per species using our 187-species HOG presence matrix; compare conserved-complex subunits vs random HOG sets of equal size and age; contrast multicellular-lineage vs sister clades (Metazoa vs Choanozoa, Embryophyta vs Chlorophyta, Dikarya vs early fungi). Use their supergroup-presence flags/DTL90_famPresent to cross-validate. Caveat: co-retention is confounded by genome completeness/BUSCO, annotation quality (transcriptome-only taxa), and by Dollo bias.
C. Elution-profile conservation, narrow version (~4-6 h). Using OG-level norm elution matrices, compare the elution peak (fraction of max / apex position within experiments) of a curated panel of ~30-50 ancient complexes (proteasome, CCT, COPI, exocyst, ARP2/3, TRAPP, COG...) between Amorphea-animal experiments, Dicty, Archaeplastida and Chlamydomonas/TSAR; test whether subunit co-elution (mean within-complex correlation) is higher in animal-only than cross-supergroup. Caveats: matrices are ENOG-merged, mix tissues/methods/fraction counts, and sampling is confounded (animals=~100 experiments, plants ~50, protists 1-3 species): conservation differences mostly reflect data volume and separation type. Restrict to within-experiment correlations and rank-based metrics; treat as descriptive. Not suited to single-origin vs independent-origin tests (only Metazoa has sister data = none).
Recommendation: do A+B (HOG level) first; C only as a small illustrative figure. Better data for animal-specific complexes would be the Skinnider/Stacey metazoan CF-MS map (Stacey/Foster lab, if available), the human complex map of Drew et al. 2021 (hu.MAP2, includes CORUM) or ComplexPortal metazoan annotations -- worth a follow-up scouting if the lead wants multicellularity-specific complexes.
