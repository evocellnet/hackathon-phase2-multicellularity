# Department-head review of the final round-2 report (report/REPORT_v2.txt)

Independent senior reviewer (Fable agent), 2026-10-30. Numbers checked against result files; figures viewed.

## Summary judgement

Round 2 did what I asked and what the lead asked. All eight of the lead's points were attempted (EukaryOMA;
GPU ProstT5 and Ancestra's structure mode; simple-to-complex singly and pooled; eight dense per-event runs;
motif-binding, cell-death and cell-type-TF sets; per-event family, architecture and recurrence tables; the
Cox map), and my priorities 1-6 and 8 were executed. The report is honest where results are thin, and
twice publishes the refutation of its own earlier claim.
I spot-checked 24 numbers across §§5-9 — the PGLS coefficient, CI, lambda and seven leave-one-out p-values;
the four confirmatory rate ratios, permutation and Stouffer p, pooled RRs; EukaryOMA agreement and
depth-adjusted expectations; 3Di 0.655/0.167/0.314; structure-mode 23.3/20.7/21.5% with CIs;
25,035/41,914/2,400/17%; OR 1.44/1.18/1.73/1.23; w6 0.110/0.076/0.074 vs shuffled 0.222/0.058/0.049;
W7 66.6/72.1/63.6%; both recurrence counts; Table 1's sums. All matched; citation fixes were applied.
Hedging is mostly correct, and in places too severe rather than too loose. The main faults are a
figure-numbering collision, one claim contradicted by the project's own record, an unacknowledged
dependence among the confirmatory events, and length.

## Must fix

1. **Figure numbering collides.** "Figure 4b" denotes both panel (b) of Figure 4 and a separate figure of
   that name. §7.3's "(Figure 4b)" points to the architecture figure, §7.4's to Figure 4 panel (b).
   Renumber the architecture figure as Figure 5 and renumber downstream.
2. **A claim contradicted by your own record (§4.1).** "EukaryOMA could not serve as a sequence source
   because its interface returns one protein per request" is wrong: results/eukaryoma_xval/summary.txt
   reports 0.50 M sequences fetched via the bulk_retrieve API. Give the real reason or drop the sentence.
3. **The four confirmatory events are not independent (§6.1-6.2, Figure 3a).** Three of them
   (*Astrephomene*, *V. carteri*, *V. africanus*) sit in one panel and share the same six controls, yet
   Stouffer's combination and the random-effects pool treat them as four independent studies.
   DESIGN_ROUND2.md §6 promised a "one effect per panel" sensitivity; it exists for the secondary set
   (results/r2/panels/summary.txt, RR 10.3, k = 6) but not for the confirmatory four. Run it, or say that
   the confirmatory p is effectively a two-panel result.
4. **State what was recommended and not done.** My priority 7 (calibration with a gene-birth simulator) was
   not attempted, yet §6.4, §10.3 and Figure 3d's 2.2-fold bias bar still rest on one round-1 simulation;
   add a sentence to §10.4. DESIGN_ROUND2 §6 also promised W1 split by overlapping and non-overlapping
   species, which §5 does not give.
5. **§7.7 and Figure 5c overstate the annotation gap.** "Most sequence-novel families carry no Pfam domain
   at all" is supported only by the two lowest events; the figure's own range runs from 1.4% (*V. carteri*)
   to 51% (land plants, 132 of 259). Quote the range.
6. **Two different "expected" agreements (§5.2 vs Figure 2 caption).** The text uses the verifier's
   24.7%/13.1%, the figure the producer's 22%. Name the estimator in each place.
7. **§2.3 item 3 is softer than the errata.** ERRATA.txt records 7 of 10 passed, 6 of 9 excluding the null
   control C15, which a method missing duplications would also pass. Give the 6-of-9 figure.

## What is good

The tip-versus-internal artefact (§6.3, Figure 3b-c) is the best self-criticism I have seen from this team:
the frozen test was kept, and the report shows reversal tips scoring 21-fold by the same comparison.
Figure 3 makes that visible in one look. The EukaryOMA comparison (§5) is the most valuable science here,
reported against the team's own interest, including the depth adjustment that dissolves its first positive
claim. The cell-death result is properly qualified: §7.3 says what the extra proteins are and refuses the
easy "cell death built multicellularity" reading. The shuffled-letter control in §8.7, and applying the
pre-registered structure-mode rule when it gave the unwanted answer, are exactly right. The
"shown / believed / not supported" box in §10.1 should be standard practice.
