ROUND-2 DESIGN REVIEW (evolutionary biology), DESIGN_ROUND2.md v0, 2026-10-29

1. STATES. "Complex" currently merges two axes: differentiated cell types and 3D tissue (Knoll 2011). Volvox (a 2D spheroid with a germ-soma split) and Nostocales (filaments with 3 or more cell types) are complex only on the first axis. Code two columns, D (germ-soma split or 3 or more cell types) and T (3D tissue with cell-cell connections). Use "D or T" as primary and run R2-P1 also with T only.
- Mycelia: simple. Fruiting bodies are complex but arose convergently 8-11 times (Nagy 2018). Flag the Pezizomycotina stem, which may hide several origins.
- Volvox is polyphyletic (Herron & Michod 2008), and Astrephomene evolved soma independently (Yamashita 2016). Astrgub is currently coded simple; recode it complex as a separate event and flag Pleodorina.
- Brown algae: Ectocarpus is filamentous. Make the parenchymatous Fucophycidae the primary event and the Phaeophyceae stem a sensitivity coding (Bringloe 2020).
- Red algae: filamentous Proteorhodophytina make "Eurhodophytina uni→simple" contested (Muñoz-Gómez 2017). Florideophyceae are complex.
- Dictyostelids: simple. Acytostelium has no stalk-cell death (Urushihara 2015), a natural contrast for the cell-death set.
- Choanoflagellate rosettes: facultative, ancestral state unresolved. Keep them as tip-level exploratory events.
- Fischerella (true branching): exploratory T event.

2. PANELS are sound. Add genome-based taxa:
- R2: Fonticula/Nuclearia (an aggregative event), Endogone sporocarps, Allomyces.
- R3: Chara (Nishiyama 2018) as a second simple→complex event; Zygnematophyceae genomes (Feng 2024).
- R4: Yamagishiella, Eudorina, Pleodorina, Volvox reticuliferus.
- R5: Galdieria/Cyanidioschyzon, Porphyridium, a filamentous proteorhodophyte.
- R6: Phaeoexplorer genomes (Denoeud 2024).
- R7: the Physarum genome; dictyostelid groups 1-4.
- R8: Pleurocapsales.

3. SETS. The ELM file has 142 unique Pfams, not 200. 24 of them come from the MOD/CLV classes (Pkinase, proteases, glycosyltransferases), which are enzymes rather than motif binders, and kinases would dominate. Restrict R2-P2 to the LIG/DOC/DEG/TRG classes, put repeat domains (WD40, TPR, ARM) in a sensitivity set, and add disorder fraction as a covariate (Niklas 2018; Pincus 2008).

Test lineage-restricted families only within the panels where they occur. Otherwise the pooled PGLS mostly measures presence/absence.

4. W6. ProstT5 predicts 3Di from sequence, so the 3Di/AA ratio partly reflects the model's mapping rather than real structural change. The ratio is also non-linear in distance and inflated in disordered regions, which are enriched in multicellular proteomes. Required changes:
- calibrate against AFDB 3Di;
- restrict to confident Pfam-domain regions;
- compare branches within matched AA-distance bins.
The expected biological signal is relaxed purifying selection under a smaller effective population size (Lynch & Conery 2003). Make R2-P5 one-sided (higher on multicellular branches) and read it together with the round-1 H9 rate result.
