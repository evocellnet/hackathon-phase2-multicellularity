# Gain-evidence ladder: prok run

Generated by `scripts/gain_ladder.py` (mcell 0.1.0); definitions in `mcell/gainladder.py` and DESIGN §3.2. Descriptive only (no hypothesis tests).

* HOGs: `runs/prok_fast/hogs.orthoxml`; tree `runs/prok_fast/stages/input/guide_tree.nwk`.
* Scope: all 43 internal branches (root stem and leaf branches excluded).
* DIAMOND: `nice -n 19 diamond blastp -q runs/gainladder/prok/queries_pass1.faa -d runs/gainladder/db/all_euk_prok.dmnd -o runs/gainladder/prok/hits_pass1.tsv.gz.part --very-sensitive -k 0 -e 1e-05 --query-cover 30 --threads 2 -f 6 qseqid sseqid pident length evalue bitscore qcovhsp --max-hsps 1 -b 4 -c 1 --compress 1`; 40929 queries, wall 10757.0 s at 2 threads. Target DB: all eukaryote + prokaryote proteomes (identical for both runs). Two-pass: 20349 rank-0 representatives, then 20580 further representatives of gains still without an outside hit.
* Deviation from DESIGN §3.2: `--very-sensitive` instead of `--ultra-sensitive` (timing pilot: ~3.5x cheaper; on the simulations it calls 1-3% more gains novel; see `recheck_ultra.json` for the real-data check).

* Ultra-sensitive recheck: of 200 random seq-novel gains (seed 11), 0 (0.0%) gain an outside-clade hit with `--ultra-sensitive`.

* **Split repair failed the DESIGN acceptance test on the simulations** (gain ratio stays 2.1-2.5x; `results/gainladder/validation_sim/summary.md`): level 2 is a diagnostic only; report levels 1 and 3. On the simulations seq-novel recall of true gains is 0.91-0.97 but precision only 0.42-0.56 (deep splits beyond detectable homology), so seq-novel counts are upper bounds.
