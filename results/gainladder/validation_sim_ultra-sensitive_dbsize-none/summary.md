## Interpretation

* **Split repair fails the acceptance test** on every simulation and preset: the rule is precise (all repairs hit spurious gains; loss/dup correlations unchanged) but applies to almost nothing, so the gain ratio stays ~2.1-2.5x. Per DESIGN, report ladder levels 1 and 3 only; repair counts are kept as a diagnostic. Most homologous spurious gains are extra paralogues of a HOG that continues into the clade (a missed duplication, not a gain+loss), and even repairing all homologous spurious gains would leave the ratio at the seq-novel level (1.6-2.3x).
* **Seq-novel is a near-lossless but insufficient filter here**: recall of true (non-HGT) gains 0.91-0.97, precision 0.42-0.59. True HGT receipts are (correctly) not novel because the donor homologue is found.
* **The simulator does not make novelty easy; it makes deep splits undetectable.** Families are independent random sequences (true gains are truly novel), but family rates ~ Gamma(2) on a height-1 tree put ~29% of families beyond ~2.5 subst./site across the root. Ancestra splits exactly these, and DIAMOND cannot see the homology either: spurious gains called novel are ~4.6-4.9 subst./site from their nearest outside relative vs ~1.7-2.7 for those detected. Real deep families keep conserved domains, so the simulator is probably pessimistic, but seq-novel counts must be read as upper bounds with a branch-length-dependent excess, compared only against matched control branches (§4.6), with extra caution on long stems and root children.
