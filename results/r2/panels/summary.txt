ROUND 2, WORKSTREAMS W2/W3: SEQUENCE NOVELTY AT EVENTS IN EIGHT DENSE PANELS
Written 2026-10-29 21:20 CET; R4_curated sensitivity added 22:03. Main result tag: results/r2/panels/pooled/final_8panels/. Definitions: METHODS_DEFINITIONS.txt,
fixed before any novelty count existed. Deviations, with what had been seen at each point: DEVIATIONS.txt (D-P1 to D-P10).
P1 event mapping: p1_mapping.txt.

WHAT WAS DONE
Eight Ancestra 'fast' panels were analysed with the round-1 code. The panels are R1 Holozoa, R2 Fungi, R3 Streptophyta,
R4 Volvocales, R5 Rhodophyta, R6 stramenopiles/Phaeophyceae, R7 Amoebozoa and R8 Cyanobacteria (18 to 40 proteomes each).
For each panel:
(1) Per-branch HOG gains, losses and duplications were counted with mcell.hogs. The conservation law held on every branch.
(2) Branch lengths were estimated on the fixed panel topology. Input: 146-150 near-single-copy root HOGs per panel,
    aligned with MAFFT and trimmed with trimAl (36-58 k sites), fitted with IQ-TREE LG+G4.
(3) The sequence-novelty ladder was run with round-1 DIAMOND settings (very-sensitive, e 1e-5, query cover 30 %, up to 3
    representatives per family, two passes). The search database held the panel proteomes plus every round-1 proteome not in
    the panel: 187 eukaryotic proteomes, or 82 prokaryotic for R8. Round-1 species that fall inside a panel clade were placed
    on the panel tree for the clade filter (round1_placement.tsv).
(4) Event and branch tables were built (per-panel event_table.tsv and branch_table.tsv).
(5) Round-1 positive controls were re-scored on each panel (positive_controls.tsv).
(6) W3: Pfam domains were collected for the families gained or duplicated on events. New species were annotated with
    pyhmmer (Pfam-A 38.2, gathering cutoffs).
Then one pooled NB GLM was fitted on 55 internal, non-crown control branches from all panels (5-9 per panel). It has a
separate intercept per panel and shared slopes for log branch length, log number of descendants, transcriptome fraction,
mean BUSCO and log distance to the nearest outgroup. The NB fit converged (alpha 0.48). Every slope was positive; four of
the five were larger than 1.96 standard errors.

CONFIRMATORY TEST R2-P1 (pre-registered; one-sided; Holm over P1-P3 is applied by the coordinator)
Question: do the four unseen simple->complex events carry more sequence-novel families than matched in-panel controls?
Per-event permutation p-values, with the number of matched controls in brackets:
  Astrephomene (tip)                       p = 0.143 [6]    rate ratio (RR) 13.2 (95 % CI 2.6-66)
  Volvox carteri (tip)                     p = 0.143 [6]    RR 14.2 (2.8-73)
  Volvox africanus/reticuliferus (node)    p = 0.571 [6]    RR 1.19 (0.23-6.2)
  Fucophycidae stem                        p = 0.167 [5]    RR 1.84 (0.30-11.2)
Stouffer Z = 1.46, combined one-sided p = 0.072. This is NOT significant at alpha = 0.05 before Holm, and so not after it.
Pooled RR (REML, modified Hartung-Knapp, q* = max(1, q)): 4.63 (95 % CI 0.58-37.0), tau^2 = 0.97, I^2 = 0.57.
95 % prediction interval: 0.03-752.
The minimum attainable per-event p is 1/6 or 1/7, because each panel has only 5-6 controls.
Depth matching was not achievable. The P1 events lie at depth 7-10, while every control in R4 and R6 lies at depth 2-6. Every
matched set therefore had to be widened to all controls of the panel (SMD of depth, event vs matched controls, 3.3-5.0).
Interpretation: there is no confirmatory evidence. The pooled point estimate is driven by the two tip events.

W3: WHAT IS GAINED AND DUPLICATED AT SIMPLE->COMPLEX EVENTS (pooled/final_8panels/w3/; per event: <R>/w3_event_families.tsv)
- Recurrence test over the 7 internal events, with matched internal controls as the null (5 seeds x 200 draws).
  Domains found in 2 or more events, counting sequence-novel and duplicated families together: 1,556 observed vs 1,057
  expected (p = 0.03). The excess comes from duplications (p = 0.02). Sequence-novel families alone show no excess:
  37 observed vs 54 expected (p = 0.76).
  After size adjustment (at most 50 annotated units per branch, rarefied; D-P8) no excess remains (p = 0.77, 0.86, 0.75).
  Events carry more duplicated families than controls, and that alone produces the apparent recurrence.
  The top 'recurrent' domains are large generic families: peptidyl-prolyl isomerase, subtilase, FAD-binding monooxygenase,
  NUDIX, EF-Tu, SNF2, P450, ABC transporters. They are not a toolkit.
- The only sequence-novel domain found in 5 or more events is the protein kinase domain (Pkinase in novel families of 5 of
  7 internal events, p = 0.023, q = 0.06). This is suggestive only.
- Event-specific signals that make biological sense:
    Volvocine events: all four (both Volvox events, Astrephomene, Pleodorina) carry pherophorin-like DUF3707
      (PF12499; the C12 family) and kinases. Both Volvox events add the volvocine matrix metalloprotease Peptidase_M11,
      and three of the four add guanylate cyclases. These are extracellular-matrix and signalling families known from
      the Volvox genome.
    Embryophyta: kinase/LRR receptor and F-box families.
    Florideophyceae: C2H2 and bZIP transcription factors.
    Fucophycidae: kinases, WD40, ankyrin and TPR families. It also has the largest duplication count of any event
      (5,924 events).
    Heterocystous Nostocales: two-component systems (response regulator, HisKA, HATPase, GAF), pentapeptide repeats and
      Uma2. These fit heterocyst signalling.
    Pezizomycotina and Agaricomycetes: MFS transporters, Zn2Cys6 transcription factors, short-chain dehydrogenases and
      CBM1. These are saprotrophic and secondary-metabolism families.
- Most sequence-novel families have no Pfam domain (for example Volvox carteri: 18 of 1,300; Fucophycidae: 63 of 493).
  Domain-level W3 statements therefore describe a small, annotatable minority.

CAVEATS
1. Controls are few (5-9 per panel). Most of each panel lies inside multicellular crowns, which the pre-registration
   excludes from the controls. Per-event p-values are coarse as a result.
2. Events are systematically deeper than controls (SMD 1.15; 3-5 for the P1 events), and the residual model cannot remove
   this. The 'burst' reading also has to beat the known stem gain inflation (2.2x rule).
3. Tip events are not comparable with internal controls: species-specific orphans inflate their counts. Use the tip-control
   or internal-only estimates instead. Tip controls exist so far only for R4 and R6. Add-on tip ladders for the other panels
   were queued (D-P6) and can be pooled with pooled.py under a new tag.
4. Novelty is judged against eukaryotic proteomes only (prokaryotic for R8), unlike round 1 (D-P3). Transcriptome species
   (R1 has 10, R7 has 9) and low-BUSCO genomes raise the novelty counts; both are model covariates, but only
   approximately.
5. The R7 Amoebozoa panel has no simple->complex event and contributes controls only.
6. One very low-occupancy taxon in the supermatrices (R2 Allomyces, 7 % of sites; R3 Spirogloea, 4 %) makes those terminal
   lengths less reliable.
7. The pooled GLM was fitted once on all panels (final_8panels). interim_5panels is superseded.
8. R3 Chara's novel families are dominated by zf-CCHC retrotransposon-like proteins. These are not in the transposon mask.
