ONE-EFFECT-PER-PANEL SENSITIVITY FOR CONFIRMATORY TEST R2-P1
Script: scripts/r2/panels_sensitivity_onepanel.py

The four confirmatory simple->complex events are not independent: three (Astrephomene, Volvox carteri,
V. africanus/reticuliferus) sit in panel R4 and are compared with the same six R4 control branches.
Here each panel contributes ONE effect:
  * R4 effect = mean residual (log rate ratio) of its 3 events. Permutation p: enumerate all C(6,3) = 20 ways of
    drawing 3 control branches without replacement from the same matched R4 control set; p = (1 + #{control mean >=
    observed mean}) / (N + 1), the same "+1" convention as the per-event test.
  * R6 effect = Fucophycidae residual and its per-event permutation p (5 matched controls).
  * Stouffer over the 2 panels; pooled RR with one effect per panel (REML tau^2, modified Hartung-Knapp, q*=max(1,q)).
Input: spec-literal (no branch-length floor) residuals from the independent verifier
  results/r2/verification/panels/verify_event_residuals.tsv, verify_control_loo_residuals.tsv
and, for comparison only, the same files under clip1e-4/ (producer's 1e-4 branch-length floor).
This is a sensitivity analysis; the confirmatory result (spec-literal Stouffer p 0.0504) is not changed.

INTERPRETATION (written by the report writer, 2026-10-30)
1. Collapsing the three R4 events into one panel effect does NOT weaken the combined test; it strengthens it.
   The R4 mean residual (1.85, RR 6.4) exceeds the mean of every one of the 20 possible triples of R4 controls
   (largest 0.59), so p reaches its floor of 1/21 = 0.048. Stouffer over R4 and R6: p = 0.031 (one-sided),
   versus the confirmatory four-event p = 0.0504. After Holm (second step, x2) this is 0.062: still not significant.
   The result is identical with or without the producer's 1e-4 branch-length floor.
2. The reason is not biological. The R4 panel effect is carried by the two TIP events (Astrephomene, V. carteri;
   RR 13.6 and 14.9), while the internal V. africanus/reticuliferus node scores RR 1.27. Ordinary unicellular tips in
   R4 already score about 6.6-fold against the same internal-control model (VERIFICATION.txt item 4), i.e. about the
   size of the R4 panel effect. Against tip controls the two tips score RR 0.56 and 0.80. The dependence-aware
   sensitivity therefore inherits the tip-versus-internal artefact; it does not provide evidence for a burst.
3. With only two panels, the pooled one-effect-per-panel RR (3.9-4.8 depending on how the within-R4 dependence is
   treated) has a 95% CI spanning roughly 0.01 to >1,000: the two-panel design carries essentially no information on
   the size of the effect.
4. The confirmatory result is unchanged: R2-P1 was not significant (spec-literal Stouffer p = 0.0504; Holm 0.10).
   Both panel p-values are at their floors (R4: 1/21 with 20 control triples; R6: 1/6 with 5 controls), so 0.031 is
   the SMALLEST two-panel Stouffer p this design can produce. The design cannot reach Holm significance (0.025 at the
   second step) whatever the data; the dependence-aware version only shows that the event ranks are at the extreme.
