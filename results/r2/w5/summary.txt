W5 - STRUCTURE (ProstT5 3Di) IN ANCESTRA AND STRUCTURAL NOVELTY OF STEM GAINS
=============================================================================
Written 2026-10-30 05:20 CET. Scripts: scripts/r2/w5/ (make_3di_fasta.py, run_r1_3di_chain.sh,
run_ancestra_patched_extra.sh, r1_compare.py, r1_verdict.py, struct_novelty.py, novelty_driver.sh,
novelty_stats.py, novelty_by_kind.py). Logs: logs/w5/. Everything here is exploratory except the
pre-registered W5 decision rule. Its reading was fixed in decision_operationalisation.txt (18:27 CET), before any
structure-rescued output existed.

MAIN POINTS
* Structure mode does not help Ancestra at the deep nodes of R1. It recovers the same deep-node controls as the
  default (C07 yes, C03 no, C06 no), and its stem events agree slightly less often with EukaryOMA.
  Verdict under the pre-registered rule: the default R1 output stays. Structure is used only post hoc.
* About half of the "sequence-novel" gained families are not structurally novel. Foldseek (3Di+AA) finds a
  structural homolog outside the clade for 32-65 % of them, depending on the panel (median about 49 %). On internal event branches the fraction that
  stays structurally new is higher than on control branches: 44 % vs 33 %, Mantel-Haenszel OR 1.44
  [1.31, 1.58]. With a branch-level permutation test, one-sided p = 0.004. After adjusting for search-space size,
  the OR is 1.45 [1.08, 1.93]. The excess comes mostly from simple->complex (nested) events, OR 1.87
  [1.63, 2.13]. Origins of simple multicellularity give OR 1.15 [1.02, 1.30].

A. ANCESTRA --family-rescue 3di ON PANEL R1 (Holozoa, 37 proteomes, 628,756 proteins)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inputs. ProstT5 3Di (own GPU implementation) for all 37 species was concatenated in Ancestra's gene order
(runs/r2/R1_3di_input/R1.3di.fa). 628,648 of 628,756 proteins (99.98 %) have a 3Di string, with 0 length
mismatches, 0 duplicates and 0 invalid letters. The 108 missing proteins are Amphimedon proteins over 3,000 aa;
the rescue skips them. Runs: runs/r2/R1_3di (--family-rescue 3di) and runs/r2/R1_3di_guard (+
--family-rescue-guard). Both use preset fast, 12 threads and the patched Leiden wrapper. Ancestra's stage keys
depend only on input md5s and configs, so the S0 families and S2 scores of the default run (runs/r2/R1) were
copied in and skipped (keys identical: input 2ba3d89c..., families b975b335..., scores ad74bdd1...). Only the
rescue, merge and output stages were recomputed. Both runs pass verify_orthoxml.

What the rescue did. It searched 128,704 small-family queries against 31,498 representatives of 3,368 large
families, with Foldseek 3Di+AA in 190 s, giving 624,381 hits. It then attached 25,035 small families (41,914
genes) to 2,400 large families. The guard confirmed only 4,180 of the 25,035 attachments (17 %) and detached the
other 20,855.

VERDICT (r1/verdict.txt). Neither variant meets either half of the rule. The deep-node controls did not improve
(1/3 = 1/3), and EukaryOMA agreement went down. The default R1 output is kept, and --family-rescue 3di is not
used for R1. R2 was not tested, as §6 of the design specifies (R1 only).

B. STRUCTURAL NOVELTY OF GAINED FAMILIES (all 8 panels)
-------------------------------------------------------
Method (struct_novelty.py). Sequence-novel gains come from the panel agent's gain ladder (read-only:
results/r2/panels/<R>/work/ladder; 'seq_novel' = no DIAMOND hit outside the clade in the panel plus 187
round-1 proteomes).
* Sampling: for every laddered branch, up to 200 sequence-novel families were drawn (seed 11). Each family is
  represented by the same <= 3 representatives the ladder used.
* Database: one Foldseek database per panel holds all panel proteins with ProstT5 3Di. Precomputed 3Di was
  imported by running `foldseek base:createdb` on the AA FASTA and on the 3Di FASTA (both with --shuffle 0, so the
  keys agree) and renaming the second database to <db>_ss. This is the same method as ancestra.structure.build_db,
  and the key order was checked against the lookup.
* Targets: for each branch, the panel proteins of all species outside the clade (createsubdb of db, db_ss and
  db_h).
* Search: foldseek search --alignment-type 2 (3Di+AA), -e 1e-3, default sensitivity, max-seqs 1000.
* Classification: a family is "structurally old" if any representative has a hit with E < 1e-3 that covers at
  least 50 % of the query; otherwise it is "structurally new".
* Search time: 2-40 min per panel on 12 CPUs.
* Events: M branches of origin, origin_complex and nested events. Internal events are the primary set. Tip events
  are descriptive only, because their gains are species-specific orphans.
* Controls: the panel agent's control branches.
* Statistics (novelty_stats.py): Fisher test per panel; Mantel-Haenszel across panels; branch-label permutation
  within panels; and a logistic GLM with log(number of outside target proteins) and panel as covariates, with SEs
  clustered by branch.

Search controls (novelty_summary/controls.tsv):
  Positive control: sequence-old gains on the same branches whose DIAMOND outside hit is a panel species (up to 60
    per branch). Foldseek finds them in 77-96 % of cases: R1 94.5 %, R2 92.4 %, R3 93.5 %, R4 80.9 %, R5 90.3 %,
    R6 77.1 %, R7 96.4 %, R8 90.9 %. Most misses are on tips and transcriptome species. The search is therefore
    slightly less sensitive than DIAMOND on some sequence-detectable homologs, mainly fragments.
  Negative control: the same queries with AA and 3Di shuffled by one shared permutation. They gave 0 hits passing
    the threshold for all 9,747 shuffled families (up to 3 representatives each) in all panels.

Sequence-novel families with a structural homolog outside the clade (all branches): R1 59 %, R2 48 %, R3 53 %,
R4 37 %, R5 50 %, R6 33 %, R7 65 %, R8 32 % (n = 2,128-8,531 per panel). The size of the sequence-novel class is
therefore roughly halved at the structure level. This is close to the round-1 hint, now without the AFDB
coverage limit.

Reading: on internal event branches, sequence-novel families are somewhat more often structurally novel than on
control branches. The effect is concentrated in simple->complex transitions. Only panel-level permutation is
robust to branch clustering, and with 2-4 events per panel no single panel is significant. The pooled estimate is
exploratory and was not pre-registered.

CAVEATS
* ProstT5 3Di is predicted from sequence. Its per-residue agreement with AlphaFold-derived 3Di is 0.655
  (w5_3di_validation), and 0.626 on non-Swiss-Prot protists. Remote structural homologs whose 3Di is badly
  predicted will be missed, so "structurally new" is an upper bound. Conversely, ProstT5 may assign similar 3Di to
  low-complexity or repeat regions; the 50 % query-coverage rule and the shuffled control (0 hits) limit this.
* "Outside the clade" means the panel only (25-40 proteomes, as specified). The sequence ladder used the panel
  plus 187 round-1 proteomes. Deep branches have few outside species: for example, the R1 Holozoa control has 3
  outside species and Opisthokonta has 1. Search-space size was adjusted for in the GLM and had no detectable
  effect (OR per log target 1.0).
* Sampling: up to 200 families per branch, so large branches are subsampled. All families of a branch share its
  history, so family-level Fisher p-values are anticonservative; use the branch-permutation and clustered-GLM
  results.
* R4: Eudosp (an AUGUSTUS proteome) was never queued for ProstT5. Its proteins are neither queries nor targets,
  which is recorded in novelty/R4/sample_info.json.
* Event branches for task B are the panel agent's event table (kinds origin, origin_complex and nested; reversals,
  sensitivity and T_event codings excluded). Controls are the panel agent's control set; branches inside crowns
  are not controls.
* Task A rests on one panel and one Ancestra preset (fast). The EukaryOMA comparison shares input proteomes with
  EukaryOMA, so it is a cross-method check and not independent data (see W1).
