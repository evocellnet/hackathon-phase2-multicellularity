1. Lineage-restriction rule (P3 pooled response; applied identically to P4; to P2 only as a sensitivity, see 4):
   A family is "widespread" and enters the pooled 187-species-tree response if EITHER
   (a) it is present (>= 1 protein with a GA hit) in >= 1 species of the multicellular clade (data/transitions.tsv, column mc_species)
       of >= 3 of the 7 primary eukaryotic origins (primary_eligible = 1: T_metazoa, T_fungi_hyphae, T_dictyostelia,
       T_streptophyta, T_rhodophyta, T_px_filaments, T_volvocine_tgv), counted over all 187 proteomes; OR
   (b) it is present in >= 50% of the species of the primary analysis set (148 species, item 2).
   Families failing both are "lineage-restricted": excluded from the pooled PGLS response and analysed only within the
   origins (multicellular clade vs sister) where they occur (per-family NB GLM of task C). Families absent from all
   species of the analysis set are reported and dropped (standardisation undefined).
   The rule is evaluated once on the primary set and the same family list is used in every sensitivity analysis
   except "repeats included" (P2) which adds the 4 sensitivity_repeat families.
2. Species (primary): kingdom = euk, data_type = genome, busco_C >= 50 (data/species_traits.tsv) -> 148 species.
   State: column `multicellular` (round-1 binary coding: simple or complex = 1; unicellular, reversals and grey-zone = 0).
   Sensitivity: grey-zone species (mc_state = grey) excluded.
3. Response: for each family f, x_sf = log(count_sf + 0.5), count = number of proteins with >= 1 domain hit of f;
   z_sf = (x_sf - mean_s x_sf) / sd_s x_sf over the species of the analysis set; y_s = mean_f z_sf (equal weight).
   Covariates: log(number of proteins in the proteome FASTA) and proteome disorder fraction (residue-weighted fraction of
   residues with metapredict score > 0.5). Model: phylolm(y ~ multicellular + log_size + disorder, model = "lambda") on
   data/species_tree_bl_euk.nwk (round-1 IQ-TREE branch lengths on the fixed guide topology) pruned to the analysis set.
   One-sided p = P(T > t) for the multicellular coefficient (t reference, n - 4 df... as returned by phylolm).
   For leave-one-origin-out the standardisation of the full primary set is kept and rows are dropped.
4. P2 primary = all 115 `set = primary` families (as frozen and as instructed); sensitivity = the item-1 rule applied to P2.
5. Leave-one-origin-out: drop all species of the multicellular clade (mc_species) of one of the 7 primary origins at a time.
6. Complex-vs-simple (secondary contrast): among multicellular species of the primary set, complex = D or T (§6):
   mc_state complex, plus the volvocines with germ-soma/soma (Volvcar, Volvafr, Astrgub; Pleosta flagged, included);
   T-only coding = round-1 mc_state complex. Same PGLS with a complex indicator (unicellular species excluded).
7. Sister-clade contrasts: per primary origin, mean y over its multicellular clade minus mean y over its sister species
   (transitions.tsv), using whichever of those species are in the analysis set (all proteomes if < 1 sister remains).
8. Scan recurrence (task C): primary recurrence = number of the 7 primary origins with BH q < 0.1 in the same direction;
   the 3 secondary nested simple->complex events (T_fungi_pezizomycotina, T_fungi_agaricomycetes, T_embryophyta) are reported
   per event and counted in a separate secondary recurrence (they are nested in primary origins, not independent).
