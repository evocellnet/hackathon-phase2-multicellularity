ROUND 2 — W4: protein-family copy number and multicellularity (comparative-methods analysis)
Produced 2026-10-29 (round-2 t ~ 0.7-1.6 h). Scripts: scripts/r2/w4/ (01-09). Rules fixed before testing: results/r2/w4/P3_rule.txt
(timestamp 2026-10-29T15:52:43+0100). Deviations and additions: results/r2/w4/DEVIATIONS.txt (D-W4-1, D-W4-2).

WHAT IS CONFIRMATORY AND WHAT IS NOT
Confirmatory (DESIGN_ROUND2 §6, tag prereg-r2): R2-P2 and R2-P3 primary PGLS only. The one-sided p-values below are raw;
the PI applies Holm across P1-P3 (P1 comes from the dense panels). Everything else here is exploratory: sensitivities, P4,
per-family PGLS, the GLMM, the Pfam-wide scans, recurrence, architectures and GO.

DEFINITIONS
- Species (primary): the 148 genome-based proteomes with BUSCO C >= 50% among the 187 round-1 eukaryotes. Of these, 68 are
  multicellular (simple or complex) and 80 are coded 0 (unicellular, reversals, and 16 grey-zone species, following the
  round-1 binary coding).
- Copy number of family f in species s = number of proteins with >= 1 Pfam 38.2 GA hit of f (pyhmmer).
- Response = equal-weight mean over families of the per-family z-scored log(count + 0.5), standardised across the analysed
  species. Units are SD of a family's log copy number. As a readable scale, the unstandardised mean log count is also reported:
  exp(coef) is then a geometric-mean fold change.
- Model: phylolm, Pagel's lambda, on the round-1 IQ-TREE tree (data/species_tree_bl_euk.nwk). Covariates are log proteome
  size (number of proteins) and proteome disorder fraction (residue-weighted, metapredict > 0.5). One-sided t-test of the
  multicellular coefficient (df = 144). The bootstrap CI is phylolm's parametric bootstrap (1,000 replicates, seed 11).
- Lineage restriction (P3_rule.txt, fixed before the test): a family enters the pooled response if it is present in the
  multicellular clade of >= 3 of the 7 primary origins, or in >= 50% of the 148 species. Under this rule P3 keeps 12 of 22
  families. Excluded as lineage-restricted: Bcl-2, BH4, BIR, CARD, Death, DED, HeLo, the two gasdermins and MazF. P4 keeps 11
  of 26 (HetR_C and PatS are absent from all eukaryotes). P2 uses all 115 primary ELM families, as frozen; the rule (94
  families) is a sensitivity.

CONFIRMATORY RESULTS
R2-P2, motif-binding domains (115 ELM LIG/DOC/DEG/TRG Pfam families). NOT SUPPORTED.
  coef = -0.028 SD (SE 0.048; 95% CI -0.124 to 0.067; bootstrap CI -0.122 to 0.065), one-sided p = 0.72, lambda = 1.00
  (bootstrap 0.91-1). Geometric-mean fold = exp(-0.019) = 0.98 (95% CI 0.92-1.05).
R2-P3, cell-death machinery (12 widespread families). SUPPORTED at raw alpha 0.05.
  coef = +0.167 SD (SE 0.064; 95% CI 0.042 to 0.293; bootstrap CI 0.047 to 0.287), one-sided p = 0.0047, lambda = 0.91
  (bootstrap 0.71-1). Geometric-mean fold = exp(0.193) = 1.21 (95% CI 1.04-1.42).
  Holm across 3 tests needs p < 0.0167 for the smallest p, so P3 would pass if it is the smallest. P1 is not known here.

SENSITIVITIES (exploratory; pgls/pgls_results.tsv; figures/forest_sets_state_effect.*)
P3 (cell death)
- Leave one origin out: the sign is positive in all 7 cases. One-sided p ranges 0.0002-0.078. The weakest case is dropping
  Streptophyta (coef 0.088, p = 0.078). Dropping Metazoa gives 0.153 (p = 0.010).
- Other variants: BM (lambda = 1) 0.136 (p = 0.019); no disorder covariate 0.178 (p = 0.003); grey-zone species excluded
  0.152 (p = 0.009); all 187 proteomes 0.149 (p = 0.005); all 187 with transcriptome and BUSCO covariates 0.179 (p = 0.001);
  all 22 families 0.138 (p = 0.008).
- Complex vs simple (multicellular species only): D-or-T coding 0.154 (p = 0.034); T-only coding 0.205 (p = 0.012).
  Three-state model: simple vs unicellular 0.111 (p = 0.073); complex vs unicellular 0.249 (p = 0.0005). The excess is
  concentrated in complex multicellularity.
- Leave one family out (post hoc, pgls/pgls_leave_one_family_out.tsv): all 12 estimates are positive, max p = 0.020.
  Without the NLR pair (NB-ARC + NACHT) the estimate is 0.136 (p = 0.018).
- Phylogenetic Poisson-lognormal GLMM (D-W4-1; MCMCglmm; 2 chains per set, seeds 11 and 23, results agree): posterior mean
  +0.47 log units (fold 1.60; 95% HPD 0.04-0.95), P(beta <= 0) = 0.02.
- Per-family PGLS (q is BH within set): NB-ARC +0.92 log (p = 0.0002, q = 0.004); caspase (Peptidase_C14) +0.35
  (p = 0.022); Death, Bcl-2, AIF_C, HET, NACHT and metacaspase are all positive. Overall 21 of 22 families are positive;
  only VPE (Peptidase_C13) is negative.
P2 (motif-binding)
- Leave one origin out: 5 of 7 estimates are negative. All p >= 0.46. Without Metazoa the estimate is -0.063 (p = 0.93).
- No sensitivity is significant: lineage rule -0.047; repeats included -0.029; all 187 -0.045; complex vs simple -0.022.
- The GLMM disagrees: +0.17 log (fold 1.18; 95% HPD -0.005 to 0.34 / 0.01 to 0.34 in the two chains), P(beta <= 0) = 0.03.
  We consider the GLMM anticonservative for many-family sets. Its phylogenetic term is a species-level effect shared by all
  families, with no family-by-phylogeny term, so each family's lineage-specific expansions count as independent evidence.
  PGLS on the per-species composite does not have this problem. We read P2 as a null with an unresolved model dependence.
- Per-family: only Sina_TRAF reaches q < 0.1 (positive). 58 of 119 families are positive.
P4, cell-type TF families (exploratory)
- coef +0.032 SD (95% CI -0.088 to 0.152), p = 0.30, lambda = 1. LOO is positive in all 7 cases, all p >= 0.08.
  Complex vs simple is negative (T-only -0.143).
- Lineage-restricted TF families tested only within the origins where they occur (scan/set_families_by_origin.tsv,
  q < 0.1): T-box (+2.1 ash log) and Runt (+0.6) in Metazoa; TEA in hyphal fungi; LOB in Streptophyta; SBP in land plants.
- Lineage-restricted cell-death families within their origins: Bcl-2, Death, CARD, DED, BIR and gasdermin are all expanded
  in Metazoa vs choanoflagellates. HeLo is expanded in Pezizomycotina; HET and NACHT are expanded there too. NB-ARC is
  expanded in Rhodophyta (Eurhodophytina). NB-ARC, TIR, TIR_2 and MACPF are expanded in land plants. Of 20 significant
  cell-death changes across origins, 19 are expansions; the exception is metacaspase, which is lower in Metazoa than in
  choanoflagellates (animals use caspases instead).
Sister-clade contrasts (pgls/sister_contrasts.tsv; mean composite in the MC clade minus sister; descriptive; 6 origins with
  sisters in the primary set):
- P3: positive in 5 of 6. Volvocines are negative (-0.19).
- P2: positive in 3 of 6 (Metazoa +0.85, Streptophyta +0.45, PX +0.28). Fungi, Rhodophyta and volvocines are negative.

PFAM-WIDE SCANS (exploratory; scan/)
Design
- 10 eukaryotic events: the 7 primary origins plus 3 nested simple-to-complex events (Pezizomycotina, Agaricomycetes,
  Embryophyta). Each multicellular clade is compared with its sister clade from data/transitions.tsv, using all data types.
- Per family: NB GLM with a log proteome-size offset; Cox-Reid profile dispersion; LRT. This was checked against statsmodels
  NB2: same MLE, slightly more conservative dispersion.
- Families: present in >= 2 species of the event, after removing 223 transposon-mask families (4,226-9,523 families tested
  per event). BH q < 0.1 within each event. Log fold-changes are shrunk with ashr (normal mixture).
Significant families per event (expanded / contracted)
- Metazoa 1,661 / 694; hyphal fungi 126 / 473; Dictyostelia 137 / 186; Streptophyta 20 / 63; Rhodophyta 11 / 34;
  PX filaments 43 / 202; volvocines 0 / 10.
- Secondary events: Pezizomycotina 38 / 19; Agaricomycetes 6 / 10; Embryophyta 264 / 250.
Recurrence across the 7 primary origins (figures/recurrence_vs_null.*)
- Observed: 42 families are expanded in >= 2 origins and 3 in >= 3; none in >= 4. 157 families are contracted in >= 2
  origins, 20 in >= 3 and 2 in >= 4.
- Pre-specified null (label permutation within events, B = 1000, seed 11): null means are 0.05 (expanded, >= 2) and 0.14
  (contracted, >= 2); every observed count has p = 0.001.
  This null is too permissive for the recurrence question. Multicellular and sister groups are distinct clades, and mixing
  them removes the divergence that makes many families significant in any clade comparison (D-W4-2).
- Added conditional null (each origin's number of significant families kept, family identity shuffled):
  - Expansions are NOT more recurrent than chance overlap: >= 2 origins, 42 observed vs 54 expected (p = 0.98); >= 3
    origins, 3 vs 0.8 (p = 0.049).
  - Contractions ARE more recurrent: >= 2, 157 vs 90 (p = 0.001); >= 3, 20 vs 3.1 (p = 0.001); >= 4, 2 vs 0.06 (p = 0.004).
Top recurrent families (scan/recurrent_families.tsv; the per-family conditional p values below are NOT adjusted for
~18,500 families)
- Expanded in 3 origins:
  - Sod_Cu (Cu/Zn superoxide dismutase): Metazoa, Dictyostelia, Rhodophyta; p = 0.002.
  - CAP (cysteine-rich secretory proteins, PR-1-like): Metazoa, Dictyostelia, Streptophyta; p = 0.001.
  - ZBED1 RNase-like: Metazoa, Dictyostelia, PX; p = 0.001.
- Expanded in 2 origins: NB-ARC (Rhodophyta and Streptophyta; also land plants), F5/F8 type C (discoidin; ELM set), RGS,
  Galactosyl_T, PTR2 and others.
- Contracted in 4 origins:
  - PDEase_I (3',5'-cAMP phosphodiesterase): fungi, Streptophyta, PX, volvocines.
  - Vint (Hint/intein-like): Metazoa, fungi, Dictyostelia, PX.

CAVEATS
1. Few origins. There are 7 primary origins, and the pooled PGLS can be carried by one lineage (Uyeda et al. 2018).
   For P3 every leave-one-origin-out estimate is positive, but without Streptophyta p rises to 0.078. The land-plant NLR
   (NB-ARC/TIR) and animal apoptosis expansions are well known. The result says cell-death families are larger in
   multicellular proteomes after phylogenetic correction, mostly in complex lineages. It does not say each origin expanded
   them independently. Sister contrasts are positive in only 5 of 6.
2. Function. NB-ARC/NACHT/TIR proteins are immune receptors as well as cell-death executors. Removing them leaves P3 positive
   (p = 0.018), but the set mixes immunity and programmed cell death.
3. Annotation bias. Pfam HMMs are built mostly from animal, plant and fungal sequences, so detection is better in those
   lineages. Many multicellular lineages are also better studied. Proteome size and disorder are covariates, but
   annotation depth is not.
4. Transcriptomes. They are excluded from the primary PGLS and included in the scans, where 6 of 8 Metazoa sisters and both
   Dictyostelia sisters are transcriptomes.
   - Several of the strongest "contractions" in multicellular clades are bacterial-like families (Bac_rhamnosid, bacterial
     surface Ig, GH43). These likely reflect prey or contaminant sequences in sister transcriptomes, or the bacterivory of
     unicellular sisters, not loss.
   - Treat recurrent contractions of glycoside hydrolases accordingly.
5. Lineage-restricted families. Animal apoptosis families (Bcl-2, CARD, DED, Death) and fungal HeLo are outside the pooled
   P3 response by rule and are reported only within origins. The pooled test therefore rests on families present in many
   lineages.
6. Scans. Sister groups are small (2-8 species), and within-event BH is applied to thousands of families with Wald-free LRTs
   at n = 6-30, so per-event false-positive control is approximate. Event species include reversals inside clades (yeasts,
   Zygnematophyceae).
7. GLMM. It is Poisson-lognormal rather than NB (D-W4-1) and anticonservative for many-family sets (see P2). It is a
   sensitivity analysis only.
