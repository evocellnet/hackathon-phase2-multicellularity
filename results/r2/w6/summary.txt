W6 (EXPLORATORY, R2-P5 moved to exploratory): does protein structure (ProstT5 3Di) change more per amino-acid change
on multicellular branches?  Panels: R1 Holozoa, R2 Fungi, R4_curated Volvocales (R4 without the two AUGUSTUS proteomes;
the full R4 lacked 3Di for Eudosp at analysis time). Other panels had <= 3 of 18-29 species with 3Di; not analysed.
Scripts: scripts/r2/w6/{w6_prep.py, w6_analyze.py, w6_figure.py}. Seed 11. <= 14 threads, nice 10.

BOTTOM LINE
No robust evidence. Inside multicellular crowns, 3Di branch lengths per AA branch length are slightly higher than on
unicellular branches in all three panels, by about 7-12 % (gene-level medians; table below). But the result fails the
phylogenetically honest test: with branches as the units (16-17 unicellular vs 5-42 crown branches), only R2 reaches
p < 0.05, and not in the ratio-of-sums version. A 3Di with no phylogenetic signal (the shuffled-3Di control) gives an
equal or larger "effect" of the same sign. Event stems (21 origins/nested events) are not shifted (12/21 positive,
sign test p = 0.33). This agrees with round-1 H9 (no >= 1.5-fold AA rate shift): neither sequence rates nor
structure-per-sequence change show a clear signature of relaxed constraint (Lynch & Conery 2003) at these transitions.

METHOD
- Genes: root-level Ancestra HOGs (runs/r2/<R>/hogs.orthoxml, via the panel cache) with one gene per species. A species
  with >1 copy is dropped from that gene (allowed in <= 10 % of species), and >= 80 % of panel species must be
  single-copy. This is the same rule as the panel phylogenomic markers. Strictly single-copy HOGs alone were too few:
  70 in R1, 21 in R2, 801 in R4_curated. Candidates: 538 / 897 / 1,438; 450 drawn at random; first 300 that pass filters
  used.
- Domains: Pfam-A 38.2 hmmsearch (pyhmmer, gathering cutoffs) on all selected proteins. MAFFT --auto on full-length AA;
  3Di letters projected onto the same columns (3Di length = sequence length was checked for every protein).
  Residues outside each taxon's own Pfam envelopes are masked in both alphabets. Columns are kept if >= 50 % of rows
  are in-domain. Taxa with < 50 % coverage are dropped. Genes need >= 50 columns and >= 8 taxa. Median 216-230
  columns, 17-35 taxa per gene.
- Branch lengths on the fixed panel topology (pruned per gene), IQ-TREE 3.1.4 -te: AA LG+G4; 3Di with a panel-specific
  exchangeability matrix + G4.
  3Di MODEL (documented deviation): IQ-TREE GTR20+FO+G4 on the concatenated 3Di did not estimate the exchangeabilities.
  On R2 all 189 stayed at the start value, so it was effectively Poisson (R2/work/model3di/gtr20.iqtree). On a subset
  it aborted with an assertion. Foldseek's mat3di.out is a log-odds scoring matrix; it is not on disk and would need a
  matrix-log conversion. So exchangeabilities were estimated per panel by Dayhoff-style pair counting: aligned 3Di
  letter pairs between taxa at AA p-distance 0.05-0.30 and 3Di p <= 0.40, r_ij = N_ij / (pi_i pi_j), mean scaled to 1.
  Empirical 3Di frequencies were used. Counts: 14.7k / 21.6k / 18.5k taxon pairs; 0.28 M / 0.49 M / 0.50 M
  substitutions. Files: <R>/work/model3di/{panel3di.paml, exchangeabilities.tsv, model_info.json}.
  Sensitivity: 3Di under Poisson+FO+G4.
- Statistic per gene and branch: y = log((L3Di*n + 0.5) / (LAA*n + 0.5)), where n = alignment columns and 0.5 is a
  pseudo-count of substitutions. 7-9 % of gene-branches have 3Di length ~0. Gene-branches are used only if the branch
  equals one guide edge, is not a root child, and has >= 1 expected AA substitution.
  Saturation/non-linearity: within tips and within internal branches separately, the AA lengths of unicellular
  reference branches are cut into deciles. Residual e = y - median(y of reference branches in the same decile).
  Sensitivity: linear residual on log LAA; per-branch ratio of sums (sum L3Di / sum LAA over genes) with a linear
  AA-length correction.
- (a) Stems of origin / origin_complex / nested events vs matched controls. Internal stems use the panel CONTROL
  branches; tip stems use unicellular non-event tips. d = stem median e minus control median. One-sided rank p.
  95 % CI from a gene bootstrap. Pooled: sign test and Stouffer across events.
  (b) Crown vs unicellular branches: per gene, mean e on crown branches minus mean e on unicellular branches, within
  tip/internal strata. Reported as the median over genes with a bootstrap CI and Wilcoxon test. Also reported with
  branches as units (Mann-Whitney on branch medians). The branch-unit version is the one to trust: genes share one tree.
- Controls:
  - Positive sanity: 3Di tracks AA length. Spearman across gene-branches 0.76 / 0.79 / 0.68; across branch medians
    0.94 / 0.97 / 0.83.
  - Constant-ratio simulation: 3Di simulated with AliSim on each gene's AA tree, scaled so that the true 3Di/AA ratio
    is the same on every branch. Panel model, the gene's fitted gamma and the real gap pattern were used, then the
    data were refitted the same way.
  - Shuffled 3Di: column-wise permutation among taxa.
  - AA split-half: two random column halves of the AA alignment.

HOW TO READ THE CONTROLS
- The constant-ratio simulation gives a small NEGATIVE crown effect (-0.03 to -0.05) and no stem shift (4/21
  positive). So the binning on a noisy AA length (regression to the mean) and saturation do not create the positive
  signal. If anything they work against it.
- The shuffled-3Di control FAILS as a negative control. 3Di with no phylogenetic signal gives a crown "effect" as large
  as or larger than the real data in R1 (0.22 vs 0.11, branch-level p = 0.026). Such 3Di behaves like near-saturated
  noise, so within an AA-length decile its length is about constant and the ratio follows where branches sit inside
  the decile. Crown branches then come out high from tree shape alone. ProstT5 3Di agrees with AlphaFold-derived 3Di
  at only 65.5 % of residues (W5), so part of the real 3Di is prediction noise of this kind. The modest positive crown
  values therefore cannot be told apart from a geometry x noise artefact.
- AA split-half is about 0 in R1/R2 but -0.28 in R4_curated (5 crown branches; regression-to-the-mean on a short-branch
  tree). This shows how unstable the crown contrast is when there are few crown branches.
- Calibration against AFDB-derived 3Di: per-species ProstT5 accuracy (W5) exists for only 4 panel species. Spearman
  with the tip residual is -0.8, p = 0.2: noisier species tend to have higher tip residuals, as the artefact reading
  predicts, but n = 4. A branch-level calibration on AFDB structures was not possible: most panel species have no AFDB
  models in our set.

CAVEATS
ProstT5 3Di is a function of the AA sequence, so the 3Di/AA ratio partly measures the model. Genes are not
independent replicates of branches: gene-level p-values (~1e-10 to 0.02) overstate the evidence. Tip stems are
compared with tips of other species, so species-level data quality (gene models, transcriptomes, ProstT5 accuracy)
is confounded with state. Single-copy genes present in >= 80 % of species are conserved housekeeping genes; relaxed
constraint, if present, may lie elsewhere. Pfam envelopes are per taxon, so domain boundaries differ slightly among
orthologs.
