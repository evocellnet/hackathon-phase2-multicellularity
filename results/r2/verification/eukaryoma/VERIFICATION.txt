INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION: EukaryOMA cross-validation (results/eukaryoma_xval/summary.txt)
=========================================================================================
Verifier run 2026-10-30. Code: scripts/r2/verification/eukaryoma/v1..v5_*.py. Outputs: this directory.
The producer's scripts were not used for the computation. I read them only afterwards, to explain differences.

OVERALL: PASS for every headline claim. One strict-criterion FAIL: 3 of 30 duplication counts on the 5 focal
branches differ by more than 5 %. All 20 agreement percentages and all pooled percentages are within ±2 pp.
The count differences come from one specific step in the producer's OrthoXML filtering, described in Task 2.
They do not change any conclusion.

TASK 1. PROTEIN MAPPING SPOT-CHECK: PASS
  * I drew 200 random mappings: 150 from the MD5 table and 50 from the RBH table. For each, I fetched the
    EukaryOMA protein from https://eukaryoma.unil.ch/api/protein/<id>/ and compared it with our sequence in
    data/proteomes/euk.
  * MD5 mappings: 150/150 sequences are identical and come from the correct EukaryOMA genome.
  * RBH mappings: 50/50 are from the correct genome, with identity >= 95.5 % (local alignment) and >= 50 %
    coverage of both proteins.
  * The mapping is one-to-one: no duplicate ids on either side.
  * 1,757,405 of 2,016,931 proteins are mapped = 87.13 %, as claimed.
  * Details: v1_mapping_spotcheck.tsv.

TASK 2. INDEPENDENT PROJECTION AND RECOMPUTATION: PASS on agreement; counts PASS except 3 duplication cells
  Method:
  * I wrote my own streaming filter (v2) for runs/euk_fast/hogs.orthoxml and for the raw EukaryOMA OrthoXML.
    It keeps the 125 species, strips TaxRange, taxonId and the taxonomy block, drops empty groups and
    collapses groups left with a single child.
  * I parsed both files with mcell.hogs on the original guide tree, which the parser prunes Ancestra-style.
  * My pruned tree is identical to runs/eukaryoma_xval/tree_pruned.nwk: same 104 internal nodes, names and
    clades. Neither parse violates the conservation law.
  * Agreement was computed my own way: majority vote of the other method's ancestral gene at the node, over
    each event's mapped members.
  Focal branches: Metazoa and Streptophyta (Klebsormidiophyceae+Phragmoplastophyta) stems; controls
  Viridiplantae, Amoebozoa, Fungi (v5_focal_branches.tsv).
  * Gains: all 10 counts (A and E) match exactly.
  * Losses: all 10 within ±3.4 %.
  * Metazoa reproduces as claimed: Ancestra 804 gains / 6,582 dups (claimed 6,617, -0.5 %); EukaryOMA 2,502 /
    196 (exact).
  * Duplications: 7/10 within ±5 %. The 3 outside are:
      - Streptophyta, Ancestra: 2,651 vs 2,343 (+13 %)
      - Viridiplantae, EukaryOMA: 254 vs 287 (-12 %)
      - Fungi, EukaryOMA: 259 vs 226 (+15 %)
  * Family-level agreement (% same), 20 values: all within ±2 pp (largest difference 1.9 pp, Amoebozoa
    E->A gains).
    Metazoa: A->E gains 21.6 % (claimed 21.8 %); E->A gains 6.9 % (claimed 6.5 %); A->E dups 0.5 %
    (claimed 0.4 %).
  * Pooled over the 6 exact internal stems vs the 26 exact controls (v5_pooled.tsv), verifier vs claimed:
      A->E gains  28.9 vs 29.0 %   (controls 10.3 vs 10.6 %)
      E->A gains   9.6 vs  9.4 %   (controls 15.4 vs 14.2 %)
      A->E dups    4.4 vs  3.8 %   (controls  4.8 vs  4.5 %)
      E->A dups    8.2 vs  7.8 %   (controls  4.9 vs  5.0 %)
    All within ±2 pp.
  Why the duplication counts differ:
  * The producer's filter (xval_lib._prune_group) does not collapse groups that are left with only one child
    after the other species are removed.
  * Its filtered files contain 106,822 (EukaryOMA) and 39,060 (Ancestra) orthologGroups whose only remaining
    child is a paralogGroup (v4_count_unary.py).
  * mcell.hogs cannot place such a paralogGroup above an orthologGroup that has the same MRCA. It therefore
    merges the paralogGroup into its parent and the duplication disappears. The parser reports this as
    n_collapsed_inconsistent: 152,549 in the producer's EukaryOMA parse vs 35,899 in mine.
  * The duplication happened among the retained species, so it should be kept. My filter collapses the
    one-child wrapper and keeps it.
  * Effect: the producer's duplication counts are biased on some branches, mostly downward for EukaryOMA.
    Examples: Diaphoretickes 151 vs 338, Alveolata, UTC clade.
  * Gains, losses and the agreement fractions are barely affected.
  * Suggested fix: collapse one-child groups when filtering, then rerun the counts.

TASK 3. ARE DISAGREEMENTS DRIVEN BY UNMAPPED PROTEINS? NO
  I restricted the analysis to events whose members are >= 80 % mapped (columns *_ge80 in v5_pooled.tsv and
  v3_per_branch.tsv). Agreement barely changes:
      A->E stem gains   28.9 -> 32.5 %   (Metazoa 21.6 -> 25.5 %)
      controls          10.3 -> 10.4 %
      E->A stem gains    9.6 -> 11.2 %
      duplications       4-9 % -> 5-9 %
  Two thirds or more of the events still disagree when nearly all members are mapped. The disagreement is
  between the methods' family structures, not a mapping artefact. Unmapped proteins lower agreement on stems
  by only about 2-4 pp.

TASK 4. IS "STEMS AGREE MORE THAN CONTROLS" EXPLAINED BY DEPTH? PARTLY (about 60 %), NOT FULLY
  * Controls sit closer to the root than stems: mean node depth 4.3 (range 2-7) vs 7.5 (range 6-9). Controls
    also have more tips: mean 13 vs 6.
  * Agreement on A->E gains rises with depth from the root: a binomial GLM on 97 non-stem internal branches
    gives +0.11 log-odds per level.
  * The GLM (covariates depth and log tips) predicts 24.7 % for the stems and 13.1 % for the controls. So
    11.6 of the 18.6-pp raw gap (62 %) is expected from depth and size alone.
  * The observed stem value (28.9 %) is still above expectation: adjusted stem log-OR +0.23, against +0.50
    unadjusted.
  * Controls of the same depth as the stems (depth 6-9, n = 8) agree 21.5 %, against 28.9 % on the stems.
  * The producer's adjusted expectation is 22.0 % and mine is 24.7 %. The difference is the GLM's baseline
    branch set (86 vs 97 branches), a modelling choice.
  * For E->A gains the stems are lower than controls (9.6 vs 15.4 %). 84 % of that gap is expected from depth
    and size.
  * Duplication agreement is uniformly low (4-8 %), and depth does not explain the small stem-vs-control
    differences there.
  * Conclusion: the producer's statement holds as written. The raw stems-vs-controls contrast (29 vs 11 %) is
    mostly a depth/size effect, and the small remaining stem excess for Ancestra->EukaryOMA gains rests on 6
    branches. Dictyostelia alone is 53 %; without it the other 5 stems give 21.3 % (302/1417), below the 24.7 % six-stem expectation. Read the
    contrast descriptively, as the summary already advises.
