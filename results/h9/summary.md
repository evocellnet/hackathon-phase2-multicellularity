# H9 — genome-wide rate shift at transitions (primary test)

Generated by `scripts/phylo/h9_summary.py` from the tables in `results/h9/`; analysis by `scripts/phylo/h9_rates.py` (run 2026-10-28T15:19:33), after PREREG hash verification (`scripts/prereg/check_prereg.py`, tag prereg-v1). Specification: DESIGN.md §4.6 H9 + v3.1; PREREG.md H9 row (`permute`, controls with two ≥2-tip child clades).

**Statistic.**
- **Per gene:** r_g = log(L̄(P→M tips)/L̄(P→S tips)), with L̄ the equal-split weighted mean path length. Branch lengths come from 250 BUSCO eukaryota_odb10 markers (IQ-TREE `-te` LG+G4 on the pruned guide tree; taxa with < 50 % site coverage removed per gene).
- **Path stems:** the D7 path stems (fungal hyphae, volvocine) include every path edge in L̄(P→M).
- **Gene inclusion:** ≥ 1 outgroup tip, ≥ 2 M tips with MRCA M, ≥ 1 S tip.
- **Per transition:** r_i = median_g r_g; 95 % CI from 2000 gene bootstraps (seed 11).

**Test.**
- **Controls:** the pre-registered pool U from `build_branch_table` (|U| = 30), restricted to nodes with two child clades of ≥ 2 tips (`h9_control_branches`). This gives **10 controls**, as assumed by the power table (pool 10); k = 10.
- **Orientation:** control r is oriented at random (`h9_values`, seed 11).
- **Null:** `permute`, 10,000 draws, seed 11, two-sided.
- **Effect:** r_i minus the mean oriented control r, pooled by REML.

Per transition, the gene-bootstrap CI excludes 0 in 6 of 7 stems, in both directions. These are rate differences between two specific clades, and the controls differ just as much (oriented control r -0.26 to 0.36).

**Stability across the pre-registered seeds [11, 23, 37, 41, 53].**
- **Permutation draws (Monte Carlo):** p 0.263–0.278.

In the genome-only rerun the stem cannot be computed at all (no S tip remains), so this value has no genome-only confirmation.

## Sensitivities (not primary; registry tier `exploratory`, `label=sensitivity`)

| analysis | n | k | T_obs | p | MC SE | pooled rate ratio [95 % CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 literal v3 'spec' scheme | 7 | 10 | 0.656 | 0.000 | 0.0001 | 1.10 [0.96, 1.26] |
| S2 genome-only taxa | 6 | 7 | 0.583 | 0.630 | 0.0048 | 1.05 [0.85, 1.29] |
| S3 control r on unicellular tips only | 7 | 9 | 0.707 | 0.146 | 0.0035 | 1.16 [1.01, 1.34] |

- **S1 (`spec`) is not informative.** All N_i are the same 10 controls. The spec null draws 7 distinct controls and can reach at most |T−½| = 0.150, below |T_obs−½| = 0.156, so p = 1/(N+1) by construction. This is the degenerate behaviour that D6 replaced, not evidence for an effect.
- **S2 (genome-only):** transcriptome tips are removed from every gene tree. Dictyostelia drops out (see above), as do the controls whose sides lose all tips (Archaeplastida, Amoebozoa, Evosea).
- **S3 (control r on unicellular tips only):** multicellular tips inside the control child clades are removed. Controls dropped: Evosea.

**Control pool and multicellular content.** The 10 controls are exactly the pre-registered pool; nothing was substituted. Each control branch is unicellular at both ends (in U). In 9 of 10, however, the child *clades* contain multicellular descendants (`h9_controls.tsv`, columns n_mc_tips1/2). Only 1 control has none, so a pool without them is untestable (≥ 2 controls needed) and would deviate from PREREG. Primary therefore = pre-registered pool with all tips; the unicellular-tips variant = S3 (confirmed by the PI).

## Caveats (DESIGN §4.6)
- **Measure.** r is a *rate shift* over the whole post-P history, not only the stem. Longer generation times bias r below 0.
- **Relaxed constraint.** "Relaxed constraint" may be claimed only with within-protein support (Kr/Kc: `results/krkc/`).
- **Equal times.** r assumes equal time from P to the M and S tips.
- **Low single-copy coverage.** Species with > 50 % duplicated BUSCOs contribute few single-copy markers (`results/qc/flags.tsv`).
