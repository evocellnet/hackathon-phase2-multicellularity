# Gene-level relative evolutionary rates (RER), 250 BUSCO markers — EXPLORATORY

Generated by `scripts/phylo/rer_summary.py` from the outputs of `scripts/phylo/rer_busco.py` (module `mcell/rates.py`; simulation tests in `tests/test_rates.py`).

**Method.** A RERconverge-style re-implementation:
- **Input.** Per-gene IQ-TREE `-te` branch lengths, ≥ 15 species per gene.
- **Normalisation.** Branch lengths are projected on a robust (median) genome-wide branch-length vector, with a square-root transform and mean–variance weighting.
- **Branch sets.**
  - *Foreground* (126 edges): the 7 primary transitions — stem edges (incl. D7 path edges) plus multicellular crown edges (within_multicellular, nested stems).
  - *Excluded* (52 edges): reversals/unicellular lineages inside those clades (yeasts, *Allomyces*, Tetrabaena stem) and every non-unicellular edge elsewhere (exploratory multicellular lineages, grey zones).
  - *Background* (159 edges): within-unicellular edges.
- **Statistic and null.** Kendall τ_b per gene. The null is a permulation by random clade placement matched on tip count (1000 permutations, seed 11; excluded edges stay excluded); p is two-sided, and BH-FDR is applied across genes with ≥ 2 transitions represented.

## Results

- **Tests.** 250 genes tested. p < 0.05: 28 (expected under the null 12.5); **q < 0.10: 0**; q < 0.05: 0. No gene has a foreground rate shift that survives FDR.
- **Calibration.** The excess of small p-values (lowest decile 45 vs 25 expected) is modest. It is compatible either with weak diffuse signal or with residual miscalibration of the clade-placement null on the real tree. We cannot distinguish the two here.
- **Direction.** Mean τ = 0.011; 57 % of genes have τ > 0. By construction RER removes genome-wide shifts, so this says nothing about H9.

Criterion A was used first. Its 5 genes (1108845at2759 (COP9 signalosome complex subunit 2), 1197019at2759 (HORMA domain), 1355894at2759 (GNAT domain), 1545004at2759 (LSM domain, eukaryotic/archaea-type), 320059at2759 (Radical SAM)) each also shift in the opposite direction in 1–2 other transitions, so they are not *consistent* shifts. Criterion B was adopted after seeing this (post hoc, stated here) and finds no gene. **Conclusion: no gene shifts consistently across ≥ 4 of the 7 transitions.**

**Caveats.**
- **Marker set.** BUSCO markers are conserved housekeeping genes, chosen for being single-copy, which is a poor set for lineage-specific rate shifts. The planned HOG-based RER (≤ 1,000 near-single-copy HOGs) needs the eukaryote Ancestra output.
- **Permulation scheme.** The clade-placement permulation is not Saputra et al.'s BM-simulation permulation (see `results/phylo/notes.md`).
