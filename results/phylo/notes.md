## Methods decisions and caveats

- **Topology fixed.** IQ-TREE 3.1.4 `-te` accepts multifurcating trees and keeps the polytomies; it did not resolve them with zero-length branches (checked on a toy tree). No `-g` constraint search was needed. The rooted guide trees are bifurcating at the root (Bikonta/Podiata; Archaea/Bacteria). An unrooted fit cannot place the root on that edge, so the fitted root edge is split equally between the two root children. Root-child branches are excluded from U, from H9 units and from RER. Lengths are mapped back by bipartition matching (`mcell/phylo.py`). Any bipartition that is missing or extra raises an error, and the topology of the output tree is asserted to equal the guide tree.
- **Markers.** Only BUSCO *single-copy* sequences are used (duplicated and fragmented ones are not). Markers are kept if present in ≥ 50 % of species. Alignment uses `mafft --auto`, trimming uses `trimal -automated1`, and the model is LG+G4 (no +F, as specified; methods review §5 suggested LG+F+G4).
- **Prokaryotes.** bacteria_odb10 and archaea_odb10 share no marker IDs. Domain-specific markers are kept at ≥ 50 % of *their* domain. Universal markers are built from reciprocal majority best-hit marker pairs (all-vs-all DIAMOND `--more-sensitive`), and the two sequence sets are merged before alignment. Only these universal markers inform the Archaea–Bacteria path. Prokaryotes are descriptive only (DESIGN v3.1).
- **Gene trees and coverage.** The "≥ 50 % site coverage" rule of M6 is applied per taxon and per gene: a taxon that covers < 50 % of the trimmed columns of a marker is removed from that gene tree before fitting. The guide tree is pruned to the remaining taxa, and each kept node carries the guide name of the MRCA of its present tips.
- **H9 (`mcell/rates.py`).** r_g = log(L̄(P→M)/L̄(P→S)), where L̄ is the equal-split weighted mean path length. Path stems (D7) sum every gene-tree edge from P to M. Tips under intermediate path nodes that fall outside M (e.g. *Allomyces*, Tetrabaena) belong to neither side. Inclusion rules are exactly those of M6, and every exclusion reason is recorded per unit × gene. Control triples use a fixed orientation; the random sign (seed 11) is applied once, by `branchstats.h9_values`. **Not run on real data** (it waits for the PREREG freeze); `scripts/phylo/h9_rates.py` refuses to run without PREREG.md and `--confirm-prereg-frozen`.
- **H9 control caveat (for the PI).** On the euk guide tree, only 10 of the 30 branches in U have two child clades of ≥ 2 tips. In 9 of these 10, the child clades contain multicellular tips; for example, Amorphea compares Amoebozoa with Obazoa (37/69 multicellular). Their r therefore mixes multicellular and unicellular rates. A sensitivity option (`unit_exclude`; `h9_rates.py --exclude-mc-in-controls`) drops multicellular tips from the control units. The default follows M6 as written.
- **RER.** This is an exploratory re-implementation of Chikina 2016 / Partha 2019 with three choices:
  - **Master branch lengths:** robust (median) genome-wide branch lengths are used instead of the mean. In simulation, 15 % of genes sharing one 3× shift biased the null genes with the mean master tree (30 % had p < 0.05); the median reduced this to 8 %.
  - **Residuals:** square-root transform, a per-gene projection on the expected path lengths, and a power-law mean–variance weighting.
  - **Statistic and null:** Kendall τ_b against foreground = stems + whole crowns. The null is a clade-placement permulation that preserves the number, sizes and clustering of the foreground clades. It is not Saputra's BM-simulation permulation.
- **Identical sequences.** IQ-TREE drops identical sequences and re-attaches them at the end as zero-length cherries, which changes a fixed topology. This happened in the first gene-tree run (e.g. *Chlarei* attached to *Astrgub*) and was caught by the bipartition check. All fits now use `-keep-ident`, and all gene trees were refitted with it. The eukaryote concatenated fit had no identical sequences.
- **Gene-tree long branches.** In 61/250 eukaryote genes (32/269 prokaryote) some branch exceeds 2 subst./site. 91 % of these long branches are terminal, and they are spread over many taxa. The most frequent are expected fast lineages (*Cyanidioschyzon*, kinetoplastids, *Plasmodium*); no single taxon dominates. `rates.h9_gene_table(max_branch=2)` can exclude these genes as a sensitivity analysis (off by default, because it is not in M6).
- **Low marker occupancy (eukaryotes).** *Spirogloea* (11 %; BUSCO D = 76 %, S = 11 %: likely uncollapsed alleles/isoforms or WGD in this genome proteome, which would also inflate Ancestra duplications on its branch, relevant to H4/H5 and to the Zygnematophyceae reversal), *Guttulinopsis* (12 %, low quality), *Selaginella* (22 %) and *Allomyces* (26 %; high BUSCO duplication, so few single-copy calls) carry few markers. In the supermatrix they are placed only by the topology, and their lengths rest on few genes.
- **H9 gene availability** (inclusion counts only; the statistic was not inspected): `results/phylo/h9_gene_inclusion_counts_euk.tsv`. The 7 primary stems have 145–250 eligible genes (lowest: Rhodophyta 145, volvocine 177). The 10 control nodes have 85–247.
- **Throttling.** Per PI instruction, steps were run with ≤ 2 single-thread jobs under `nice -n 19` from 14:26 onward. The eukaryote step 1 started with 4 jobs and was restarted, and completed alignments were reused.
