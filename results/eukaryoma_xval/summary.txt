EUKARYOMA CROSS-VALIDATION OF ROUND-1 ANCESTRA STEM EVENTS
==========================================================
Generated 2026-10-29 by scripts/eukaryoma/* (order: build_species_map, fetch_eukaryoma_md5, map_proteins,
fetch_unmapped_seqs, rbh_map, prepare_inputs --step tree|ancestra|ancestra_hints|eukaryoma, branch_mapping,
xval_analysis, positive_controls, h1h2_descriptive, summarize). Tests: tests/test_eukaryoma_xval.py (4 known-answer
tests: tree pruning, branch mapping, OrthoXML filtering and parsing, gain and duplication classifiers).
Inputs: EukaryOMA release All.Oct2026 (FastOMA 0.5.1 OrthoXML, md5 296586b7...); round-1 Ancestra run runs/euk_fast.

MAIN POINT
----------
The two methods agree on the origin of only a minority of stem gene families and duplications. That is also
true on control branches, so agreement is not worse on stems; for Ancestra's stem gains it is better than on
controls. The two methods split events differently: on the animal stem, Ancestra reports 6,617 duplications
and 804 new families, while EukaryOMA reports 196 duplications and 2,502 new families. A stem "gain" or
"duplication" count from either method therefore says how that method splits families, not only what happened
biologically. The round-1 H1/H2 direction is reproduced (Ancestra-on-the-same-tree T = 0.72; EukaryOMA T = 0.63;
0.5 = no effect). One round-1 positive-control failure (C07, T-box duplications) turns out to come from Ancestra's
level annotations rather than from its gene families: with the purely topological placement used here, Ancestra
passes C07. EukaryOMA passes none of C03/C06/C07.

1. SPECIES AND PROTEIN MAPPING
------------------------------
* 125 of our 187 species have a EukaryOMA genome (species_map.tsv). 93 match by taxid and name. 24 match by
  binomial name only, because our strain-level taxid differs from their species-level one. 5 match by taxid only
  (synonyms: Pyropia/Neopyropia yezoensis, Chromosphaera perkinsii = "Ichthyosporea sp. XGB-2017a",
  Paraphelidium tribonemae/-matis, Corallochytrium and Ministeria strain names). 3 were added by hand at species
  level (Volvox carteri f. nagariensis -> V. carteri; Trypanosoma brucei brucei -> T. brucei; Thecamonas trahens
  ATCC 50062 -> T. trahens). All were checked by name; the protein mapping below confirms most of them.
* The EukaryOMA OrthoXML carries only internal OMA ids (protId X2599xxxxx) and no UniProt/EukProt accessions.
  Proteins were therefore mapped by identical sequence: the MD5 of our sequence against the sequence_md5 that the
  REST API reports for all 2.31 M proteins of the 125 genomes. This mapped 1,609,173 of 2,016,931 of our
  proteins (79.8 %; median species 99.3 %).
* Second pass for the 28 species with < 90 % MD5 mapping, which come from different assemblies or gene-model
  versions: 0.50 M EukaryOMA sequences were fetched (bulk_retrieve API), and reciprocal best DIAMOND hits were
  accepted at >= 95 % identity and >= 50 % coverage of both proteins. This added 148,232 proteins, for a total of
  1,757,405 / 2,016,931 = 87.1 % of our proteins mapped (protein_mapping_stats*.tsv). 15 species stay < 50 %
  mapped, including the metazoans Ephydatia, Mnemiopsis, Nematostella and Hydra (34-42 %), Mesostigma (4 %,
  EukaryOMA has 6.3 k proteins vs our 20.8 k) and Pythium oligandrum (3 %). Unmapped genes still count in each
  method's own event counts. They only limit the family-level matching.

2. PROJECTION ONTO A COMMON TREE (both methods scored the same way)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
* Tree: our guide tree pruned to the 125 species (runs/eukaryoma_xval/tree_pruned.nwk; 104 internal nodes). A
  node is kept if >= 2 of its children contain kept species, and it keeps its original name.
* Both OrthoXMLs were restricted to these species, and empty groups were dropped. Every level annotation was
  stripped (TaxRange and taxonId), and both were parsed with the unchanged round-1 parser (mcell.hogs).
  Placement is therefore purely topological and identical for the two methods: an orthologGroup sits at the MRCA
  of its members, a paralogGroup on the branch above that MRCA, and a family is "present at X" when its gene tree
  crosses X. This is the "spans the two child clades" definition. EukaryOMA's own TaxRange levels were not used,
  because they refer to a different species tree. Conservation law: 0 violations in both parses.
* Sensitivity: keeping Ancestra's TaxRange annotations (variant ancestra_hints) changes stem counts by < 15 %.
  For example, Metazoa duplications are 6,637 vs 6,617.
* Branch mapping (branch_mapping.tsv): a stem is "exact" when the pruned branch or path covers exactly the
  original edges.
  - Exact: 8 of 21 primary/secondary stems. These are Metazoa, Dictyostelia, Streptophyta (Klebsormidiophyceae
    + Phragmoplastophyta), Eurhodophytina, Rhodymeniophycidae (Florideophyceae), PX clade, and the tip stems
    Gonapodya and Fonticula.
  - Approx: 7, flagged and not pooled. Fungal hyphae maps to Eumycota>Dikarya because no Mucoromycota or
    Zoopagomycota are sampled. The volvocine, Phaeophyceae and Pezizomycotina stems collapse onto single-species
    tips (Volvox carteri, Ectocarpus, Neurospora), so "supported" gains are undefined there.
  - Missing: 6 (Neolecta, Acrasis, Copromyxa, Tetrabaena, Chara, Ulva).
  - Of the 7 round-1 primary stems, 5 are exact.
  - Controls: 26 of the 30 round-1 control branches are exact. A further 60 exact internal branches serve as a
    second baseline.

3b. FAMILY-LEVEL AGREEMENT (agreement_events.tsv.gz, agreement_per_branch.tsv, agreement_pooled.tsv,
    agreement_stems_depth_adjusted.tsv)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Method: each event is matched in the other method through its member proteins, and the other method's ancestral
gene is taken at the gain node by majority vote.
* Gains are classified as same (the other method also places the origin on the stem path), deeper (it places the
  origin at or above the stem's parent: an older family, so an Ancestra gain classed "deeper" is a split of an
  older family, i.e. a spurious gain), shallower (no mapped member has an ancestral gene at the node in the other
  method), or unmapped.
* Duplications are classified as same (>= 2 copies map to distinct genes at the node that share one ancestor at
  the stem's parent), deeper (the copies were already separate before the stem), shallower (a single gene at the
  node, i.e. no duplication on the stem), or other.

Pooled over the 6 exact internal stems (tip stems excluded), compared with the 26 exact controls:
  Ancestra gains -> EukaryOMA: same 29.0 % (540/1859 mapped), deeper 18.6 %, shallower 52.4 %. Controls: same
    10.6 %, deeper 55.4 %. The depth- and size-adjusted expectation for these stems is 22.0 % (binomial GLM on
    log tips and depth over 86 non-stem branches).
  EukaryOMA gains -> Ancestra: same 9.4 % (542/5750), deeper 88 %. Controls: same 14.2 %; adjusted
    expectation 11.9 %.
  Ancestra dups -> EukaryOMA: same 3.8 % (153/3992). Controls: 4.5 %. Most Ancestra duplications are
    "shallower" (one EukaryOMA gene at the node) or "other".
  EukaryOMA dups -> Ancestra: same 7.8 % (166/2120). Controls: 5.0 %.
* Most disagreements are large. When one method calls a stem gain and the other places the family deeper, the
  median offset is 8 nodes, close to the eukaryotic root. This points to deep over-merging or stray members, not
  to a one-node shift. About 78 % of Ancestra stem gains classed "shallower" have mapped members that EukaryOMA
  places in no multi-species group among the 125 species. Of these members, 34 % (Metazoa check) are in no
  EukaryOMA HOG at all. The rest are in HOGs restricted to species outside our set.
* Per transition:
  - Highest gain agreement (Ancestra -> EukaryOMA): Dictyostelia (53 % same; 99th percentile of baseline
    residuals) and the approx Agaricineae (68 %).
  - Lowest: PX clade (4 %; 5th percentile) and Eurhodophytina (10 %).
  - Metazoa: 22 % of Ancestra's 804 stem gains are confirmed (expected 21 %), 7 % of EukaryOMA's 2,502, and only
    5 of Ancestra's 1,380 Metazoa-stem duplication events (0.4 %). 930 of them are a single gene at Metazoa in
    EukaryOMA.
* Is agreement worse on stems? No. Ancestra stem gains agree with EukaryOMA more often than control gains
  (29 % vs 11 % raw; 29 % vs 22 % after the depth/size adjustment). EukaryOMA stem gains agree slightly less
  (9 % vs 12 % adjusted). Duplication agreement is uniformly low (4-8 %) on stems and controls alike. The raw
  stems-vs-controls Fisher tests (agreement_pooled.tsv) are strongly "significant", but they are confounded by
  depth: controls are deeper branches where both methods disagree more. Read them descriptively only.

4. POSITIVE CONTROLS C03 / C06 / C07 (positive_controls.tsv, positive_controls_diagnostics.tsv)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The round-1 rules were applied unchanged, with the pre-registered Pfam subset (the PF-HOG 50 % rule uses
mapped EukaryOMA members only), on the 125-species tree:
                        round 1 Ancestra   Ancestra (pruned, topological)   EukaryOMA (pruned)
  C03 cadherin cyto     no (a fails)       no (a fails)                     no (a fails)
  C06 integrin beta     no                 no                               no
  C07 T-box             no (c fails)       YES (9 dups on Metazoa stem)     no (a, b, c fail)
  (C01, C02, C04 pass in all three)
* C03: in both methods the PF01049 lineage originates before Metazoa. In EukaryOMA it is one Choanozoa-level
  PF-HOG that includes choanoflagellate cadherins. This is the "domain gained inside an older lineage" limitation
  of any HOG-level rule. It is not specific to Ancestra.
* C06: in both methods integrin-beta PF-HOGs root at Eukaryota/Podiata in over-merged EGF-rich families. The
  loss sub-rules fail as in round 1 (Fonticula and Didymoeca, see failure_diagnosis.md).
* C07: in EukaryOMA the T-box genes form 11 separate families, most originating at Metazoa. No lineage spans
  Ichthyosporea and Metazoa, so the co-option component (a) fails. With level annotations stripped, Ancestra
  places 9 T-box duplications on the Metazoa stem. The round-1 failure (duplications on the Choanozoa stem) was
  therefore caused by Ancestra's paralogGroup taxonId placement, not by its gene families. In the pruned topological
  parse Ancestra still has 4,318 duplications on Filozoa>Choanozoa (EukaryOMA: 222), so the "too-deep"
  duplication excess is only partly a level-annotation effect.

5. ROUND-1 H1/H2 RECOMPUTED DESCRIPTIVELY (h1h2_descriptive.tsv, h1h2_descriptive_T.tsv)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same machinery as round 1 (NB-GLM residual on control pool, u = rank among the round-1 matched controls, T =
mean u), but y = supported HOG gains on the pruned tree, the covariates are recomputed on the pruned tree, and
the pool is the 26 exact controls. No p-values are computed.
  T over the 5 exact primary stems: Ancestra-pruned 0.717, EukaryOMA 0.633; round-1 (sequence-novel gains, 187
  spp) on the same 5 stems 0.710 (all 7: 0.711). Adding the approximate fungal stem gives 0.661 / 0.572 / 0.675.
  Per-stem residual signs, EukaryOMA vs round 1:
    agree (4 of 5 exact stems): Metazoa (+0.94 vs +0.88), Dictyostelia (+0.84 vs +0.23), Streptophyta
      (+0.71 vs +0.06), Eurhodophytina (-0.94 vs -0.37)
    disagree: PX clade (-0.90 vs +0.51). Ancestra on the pruned tree also gives -0.63 here, so the sign change
      comes from the 125-species sampling (only 2 PX species retained), not from the method.
    Fungal hyphae (approx): negative in all three.
  The direction agrees and the size is similar to slightly smaller (T 0.63 vs 0.71). This is consistent with the
  round-1 conclusion of no clear excess of new families on multicellular stems. The EukaryOMA T is closer to 0.5.

CAVEATS
-------
* EukaryOMA is not ground truth. FastOMA and Ancestra fail differently: FastOMA leaves 53 % of genes outside
  multi-gene groups here and splits paralogs into separate root HOGs; Ancestra over-merges and over-calls
  duplications. "Agreement" is a consistency measure, not accuracy.
* Different input proteomes. 13 % of our proteins are unmapped, concentrated in 15 species (incl. 4 metazoans),
  and EukaryOMA proteomes for some species are larger (e.g. Ephydatia 56 k vs 37 k).
* Only 125/187 species. Sparse sampling inside clades merges branches (approx stems) and moves some events onto
  neighbouring branches. The numbers here are not the round-1 numbers. The pruned-tree Ancestra parse is the
  like-for-like comparison.
* The topological placement ignores both methods' own level calls. For Ancestra this removes the paralogGroup
  taxonId placement that caused C07's round-1 failure. For EukaryOMA it ignores a species tree that differs from ours.
* Stems vs controls differ in depth and size. The GLM adjustment is simple (2 covariates, 86 baseline branches).
  The per-stem percentiles rest on single branches.
