# Figure captions

**Figure 1. The eukaryote guide tree, its 187 proteomes and the seven primary transitions.** Branch order and grouping only; branch lengths are not drawn. Tip symbols give each species' state: unicellular (grey circle), simple multicellular (blue circle), complex multicellular (blue square), grey zone such as colonial, coenocytic, plasmodial or facultative forms (orange diamond), and reversal to unicellularity (green triangle). An asterisk marks the 36 proteomes derived from transcriptomes. Thick black segments are the stems (P → M) of the seven pre-registered primary transitions, numbered as in the key and in Table 1. Stems 2 (fungal hyphae) and 7 (volvocine colonies) are two-branch path stems. Light shading marks each multicellular crown clade M. Outer arcs label major clades. Sources: tree data/species_tree_bl_euk.nwk (topology identical to data/guide_tree_euk.nwk); states data/species_traits.tsv; stems data/transitions.tsv. Script: scripts/report/fig_trees.py.

**Figure 2. Workflow.** (1) Proteomes, guide trees and the transitions table. These inputs were frozen and hashed, together with the design, test specifications and power table, before any eukaryote output existed. (2) Ancestra reconstructs hierarchical orthologous groups (HOGs). A parser turns them into per-branch gains, losses and duplications. Gains are ranked on the gain-evidence ladder, because raw HOG gains were inflated 2.1–2.6-fold on simulations. Pfam domains and disorder annotate proteins, and 250 BUSCO marker genes give branch lengths and gene trees. (3) Seven primary stems are compared with a pool of 30 unicellular control branches in four gene-content tests (H1/H2, H4, H5, H12) and one rate test (H9), with Holm correction across the five tests. Analyses in the dashed box are exploratory. Script: scripts/report/fig_workflow.py.

**Figure S1. The prokaryote guide tree (82 genome-based proteomes).** Symbols as in Figure 1 (there are no reversals or transcriptomes). The four numbered stems are the secondary prokaryotic transitions. These are analysed descriptively only, because the tree yields just 2 eligible control branches. Sources: data/species_tree_bl_prok.nwk; data/species_traits.tsv; data/transitions.tsv. Script: scripts/report/fig_trees.py.
