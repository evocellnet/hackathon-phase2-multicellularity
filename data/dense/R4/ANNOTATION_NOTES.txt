R4 ANNOTATION NOTES - de novo gene prediction for unannotated volvocine assemblies

Why: Yamagishiella unicocca and Eudorina sp. (undifferentiated Volvocaceae; needed as simple-state relatives of the differentiated Pleodorina/Volvox events) have public assemblies (Volvocales Genome Consortium) but no published gene annotation in NCBI, UniProt 2026_03, EukProt v3 or LukProt v1.5.1 (checked 2026-10-29).

Method (scripts/dense/augustus_predict.sh): AUGUSTUS 3.5.0 (conda_envs/augustus, bioconda; libboost 1.85) ab initio, species model chlamy2011 (Chlamydomonas reinhardtii, shipped with AUGUSTUS), no hints/RNA-seq/protein evidence, --gff3=on --protein=on --uniqueGeneId=true --alternatives-from-sampling=false (one transcript per gene), both strands, partial genes allowed (default). Assemblies split into 7 length-balanced chunks run in parallel (nice 10). Proteins taken from the GFF3 protein comments, then the round-1 cleaning (terminal stop trimmed, non-standard -> X, >= 50 aa, exact duplicates removed, IDs <code>_<chunk>_<gene.t1>).

Runtime: AUGUSTUS run 16:25-17:21 (Yamagishiella, 134 Mb) and 16:25-17:29 CET (Eudorina, 169 Mb) on 7 chunks each (14 cores, nice 10); a first attempt (15:55-16:25) was killed by the session's background time limit and restarted from scratch.

Caveats: ab initio predictions without evidence miss/merge/split genes and miss lineage-specific genes poorly captured by the Chlamydomonas model; gene-content differences involving these two proteomes must be checked with a sensitivity run without them (species.tsv column annotation_source == augustus_denovo).
